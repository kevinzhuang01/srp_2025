## Academic Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and medical imaging. I focus on developing self-supervised and data-efficient learning methods that leverage clinical imaging and genetic data for disease diagnosis, prognosis, and staging. While much of my work emphasizes lung disease, particularly COPD, the approaches I develop are broadly applicable to precision medicine. Beyond my doctoral research, I am currently interning with a Department of Defense contractor. Although this work differs from my healthcare-focused studies, it has strengthened core research skills such as data cleaning, image preprocessing, and statistical analysis, while also exposing me to new domains of machine learning, including reinforcement learning. This experience has broadened my perspective and sparked my interest in defense-related applications alongside healthcare. My long-term goal is to solve problems in high-impact environments by advancing cutting-edge research and developing innovative AI tools that benefit both science and society.
